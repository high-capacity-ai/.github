# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability
Please do not open a public issue for a security vulnerability.

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the affected repository (Security tab -> Report a vulnerability), or email chris.turnbull@highcapacity.us.

We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 business days.

## Scope
This policy covers the public repositories of the High Capacity AI organization. Client engagements and private repositories are governed by their own agreements.
