# High Capacity AI

Managed AI services and operational relief exclusively for solopreneurs and owner-led (1–5 person) businesses. Not an IT consultancy; not enterprise bloat.

## How We Deliver

Client outcomes are delivered through a proprietary, human-commanded multi-agent operating system organized into four functional units: **the Influencers** (market and communications), **webHeadz** (digital surfaces), **Data Science Team** (evaluation and intelligence), and **Platform Wizards** (infrastructure and orchestration). Every AI work lane passes structured evaluation gates before it touches production client work, and a human executive approves every external action.
