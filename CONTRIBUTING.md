# Contributing

High Capacity AI's public repositories are published frameworks and patterns, maintained by the High Capacity AI teams. They are not open task queues.

## How changes happen
- All changes land through pull requests reviewed against the repository's PR template fence checks. Direct pushes to main are not accepted.
- Work is planned and tracked in High Capacity AI's internal system of record. GitHub Issues on public repositories are a curated public roadmap, not a task inbox — please don't open issues to request work.
- Corrections and suggestions: email chris.turnbull@highcapacity.us.

## Standards for anything published here
- No client names or client material without written consent.
- No prompts, datasets, configurations, scripts, or internal mechanics — principles and frameworks only.
- Documents follow the YYYY-MM-DD__Subject__Descriptor.md naming convention; releases follow Semantic Versioning.
